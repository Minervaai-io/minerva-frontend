Why Phuket

The numbers speak
for themselves.

Phuket's cash-based, tourism-driven property market has outperformed mortgage-dependent resort markets for over two decades.

01

Cash-based stability

Over 90% of transactions are cash. No mortgage bubbles, no credit crunches. Phuket's market stayed stable through 2008 and COVID while Mallorca crashed.

02

8–10% price growth

Premium properties are appreciating 8–10% annually, with luxury beachfront villas seeing up to 15% year-on-year growth in top locations.

03

Strong rental yields

Well-managed villas and condos deliver 6–8% net rental yields, driven by 4 million+ international tourists annually and growing digital nomad demand.

04

Foreign ownership

Foreigners can own freehold condominiums outright. Villas are accessible through established leasehold and company structures used for decades.

05

Limited prime supply

Beachfront and west-coast land is running out. Scarcity is driving prices up in Bang Tao, Kamala, and Surin. The window is narrowing.

06

Lifestyle + returns

World-class beaches, international schools, premium healthcare, and year-round sunshine. Your investment is also your escape.

Frequently asked

Common questions
from buyers.

Can foreigners own property in Phuket?

Yes. Foreigners can own freehold condominium units outright (up to 49% of a building's total area). For villas and land, established structures include 30-year renewable leaseholds and Thai company structures. We connect you with experienced property lawyers to set up the right ownership model.

What are typical rental yields in Phuket?

Well-located, professionally managed properties typically deliver 5–8% net annual rental yield. Premium villas in Bang Tao and Surin can exceed 8% during peak season. Returns depend on location, management quality, and pricing strategy.

Do I need to visit Phuket to buy?

Not necessarily. Many international buyers complete purchases remotely using virtual property tours, video consultations, and power of attorney arrangements. However, we always recommend visiting if your schedule permits. Nothing replaces seeing a property in person.

What is the buying process timeline?

From initial inquiry to completed purchase typically takes 4–8 weeks. This includes property selection (1–2 weeks), due diligence and legal review (1–2 weeks), and transfer completion (2–4 weeks). Off-plan purchases may have different timelines based on construction schedules.

Are there financing options for foreign buyers?

Most foreign purchases in Phuket are cash transactions, as Thai banks generally do not offer mortgages to non-residents. Some developers offer installment plans during construction. A few international banks may provide financing against assets in your home country.

What ongoing costs should I expect?

Typical ongoing costs include common area maintenance fees (condos), property management fees (if renting out), annual insurance, and minor local taxes. Total ongoing costs typically range from 1–3% of property value annually, depending on the type and management arrangement.
